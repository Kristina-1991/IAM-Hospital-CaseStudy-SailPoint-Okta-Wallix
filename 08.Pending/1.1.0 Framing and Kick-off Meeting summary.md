# Framing and kick-off Meeting summary
## Meeting context 

This first meeting is initiated by the IT Security Department of CHR Brussels, under the sponsorship of the Chief Information Officer (CIO), and facilitated by the Project Manager.

**Objective:*** Align multidisciplinary stakeholders on audit findings, project scope, and governance; initiate Project Charter and BRD v0.1 drafting.

**Participants**
- Sponsor: CIO (CHR Brussels)
- Core Team: Project Manager, IAM Business Strategic Analyst (Consultant), IAM Architect, Solution Architect
- Key Stakeholders: IT Security Lead, DPO (Compliance), HR Representative, Clinical Application Owners

**Key Outcomes**
- Reviewed pentest findings and translated into prioritized business/security risks.
- Defined project governance (RACI, communication plan, milestones).
- Initiated IAM solution mapping (SailPoint → IGA, Okta → IDP, Wallix → PAM).
- Drafted initial business requirements and success metrics.
- Scheduled formal Kick-off Meeting for validation and sign-off of Project Charter and BRD v0.1.
