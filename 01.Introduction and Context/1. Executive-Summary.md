# Executive Summary 

The Brussels Regional Hospital Center (CHR) is modernizing its IAM ecosystem to strengthen patient data protection, operational efficiency, and regulatory compliance amid rising cyber threats in healthcare.

Triggered by a cybersecurity audit revealing critical gaps in identity lifecycle management, governance, and privileged access, CHR allocated €500,000 to implement a Zero Trust-aligned IAM platform integrating:

- **SailPointIQ for IGA** (lifecycle automation, SoD, access reviews), 
- **Okta for IDP** (SSO and adaptive MFA),
- **Wallix for PAM** (privileged session monitoring and traceability). 

## Key Business Objectives 

- Enforce least privilege and robust controls for sensitive healthcare data.
- Automate provisioning/deprovisioning via HR integrations (ULIS, Foederis).
- Secure clinical systems (OASIS+, QBLOC, DXPlanning) while optimizing patient care workflows.
- Achieve full compliance with GDPR, NIS2, ISO 27001/27799, and eHealth Belgium standards.
