# Business Drivers & Problem Statement 

## Core Clinical Systems supporting the patient Journey

CHR Brussels relies on three mission-critical applications:

- **DXPlanning** – Appointment scheduling, resource allocation, and patient flow management with HL7 interoperability.
- **QBLOC** – Surgical block planning, operating room coordination, pre/per-operative documentation, and traceability.
- **OASIS+** – Centralized electronic patient record consolidating clinical history, lab results, admissions, and decision support.

Access to these systems is currently managed manually via IT tickets, with no centralized IAM platform.
