# Audit and Challenges
An independent penetration test exposed vulnerabilities in manual IAM processes, including orphan accounts, absent MFA, uncontrolled privileged access, and non-compliance with ISO 27799 healthcare data protections.

Key impacts: 

| **Challenge** | **Description** | **Impact** |
|------------------|----------|----------|
| Lack of IGA Governance| Slow, manual onboarding/offboarding, no segregation of Duties, absence of certification campaigns and access review processes| Orphan accounts, excessive access rights, regulatory non-compliance| 
|Absence of MFA| No multi-factor authentication on clinical applications| Elevated risk of unauthorized access and data breaches|
| Uncontrolled Privileged Access| Shared or unmanaged privileged accounts with no traceability| High risk of misuse and absence of audit trails|
| Non-compliance with ISO 27799| Lack of specific controls to protect healthcare data per ISO standards | Increased legal and regulatory exposure|

## Objectives & success criteria 

- Protect patient data via least privilege and automation.
- Boost efficiency with 100% automated IGA (on/offboarding).
- Deploy MFA for all clinical users; reduce access incidents by 80%.
- Ensure full PAM traceability and regulatory alignment.

## Scope  

### In scope
- HR integrations (ULIS, Foederis)
- secure clinical apps
- Zero Trust governance
- access review workflows.

### Out of scope
- New HRIS/clinical app development
- unrelated network upgrades
- non-standard third-party integrations.

### Key deliverables 
- Zero Trust IAM Architecture
- BRD
- Use Case Catalog & Diagrams
- Compliance Mapping; Deployment/Governance Playbooks.
