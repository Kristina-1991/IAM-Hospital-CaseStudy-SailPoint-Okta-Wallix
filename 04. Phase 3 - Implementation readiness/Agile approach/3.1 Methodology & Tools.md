# Methodology and tools 

## Methodology adopted
The IAM CHR Brussels project adopts a hybrid Agile approach (Scrum) adapted to a regulated hospital environment:

- Scrum for design, integration development and testing activities (short cycles, incremental delivery).
- Kanban for managing governance flows (access reviews, compliance mapping) and change requests.
- Regulatory gates (DPO sign-off, IT Security, Clinical Owners) at the end of each macro-phase to comply with NIS2 and ISO 27001.

## Duration & Frequency

Sprint duration: 2 weeks
Total Phase 3: Weeks 9–11 (6 sprints)
Daily Stand-up: 15 min (Teams)
Sprint Review + Demo: End of sprint (with clinical stakeholders)
Retrospective: Internal project team
