# Hih-level Project Timeline 

The program follows a 15-week phased approach with progressive delivery and stakeholder validation gates.

|Phase |	Duration|	Key Objectives|	Major Deliverables|	Milestone|
|------|----------|---------------|-------------------|-----------|
|1. Analysis & Definition|	Weeks 1–4	|Capture requirements, risks, governance|	Project Charter, BRD v0.1, RACI, Risk Register|	BRD v0.1 Draft & Governance Sign-off|
|2. Design & Validation|	Weeks 5–8|	Define target architecture & processes|	Target Architecture, BPMN TO-BE, Role Model, BRD v1.0|	High-level Architecture & BRD v1 Sign-off|
|3. Implementation Readiness|	Weeks 9–11|	Prepare testing and access governance	| UAT Plan, Access Review Process, Test Cases|	UAT Readiness|
|4. Go-Live Preparation |	Weeks 12–13|	Finalize rollout and training |	Deployment Strategy, Training Plan|	Go-Live Approval|
|5. Handover & Governance |	Weeks 14–15|	Transition to operations |	KPI Dashboard, Governance Playbook |	Project Closure & Handover |

**Critical Dependencies** 

- Early HRIS (ULIS/Foederis) data access for provisioning design.
- DPO sign-off on all patient-data access models.
- Clinical stakeholder availability for workshops and UAT.
